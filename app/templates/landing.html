Payroll software for Ghanaian businesses

Payroll for Ghana, from spreadsheet to payslip.

{{ app_brand_name }} works out pay, PAYE and SSNIT from the Excel workbook you already keep, takes each run through approval, and sends every employee their payslip by email, WhatsApp or SMS.

Who uses it

Businesses running their own payroll

Five staff or five hundred. Upload the month's hours and pay, check the figures, approve, and send the payslips.

Payroll bureaus and accountants

Run payroll for many client companies from one account. Each client gets its own portal and sees only its own people.

Groups with several companies

Separate books for each company, and one place to see payroll cost and what still needs approving.

How it works

Four steps, every payday.

  1. 1

    Upload

    Upload your Excel workbook. Each row is matched to an employee, and anything that doesn't fit is flagged.

  2. 2

    Calculate

    Gross pay, PAYE, SSNIT and deductions, at the GRA rates in force for that month.

  3. 3

    Approve

    Check the totals and flagged rows, then approve. Who approved it, and when, goes on the record.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    Payslips go out to each employee, and you can see which ones arrived.

Approving a run records the decision. {{ app_brand_name }} doesn't move money; you pay staff through your bank as usual.

Features

What's included

PAYE and SSNIT

Current GRA PAYE bands and SSNIT rates, kept as dated versions, so an earlier month always recalculates the same way.

Checks before approval

Each run is compared with the last one. Large pay changes, headcount changes and duplicate rows are held for review.

Payslip delivery

Email, WhatsApp and SMS, with a delivery status for every employee and one-click resend for any that failed.

Reports

A bank listing and the GRA PAYE schedule for every approved run, ready to download.

Employee records

Staff details, bank or MoMo accounts, SSNIT and TIN numbers, and documents, kept in one roster.

Audit trail

Every upload, edit, approval and send is logged with who did it and when.

Common questions

How much does {{ app_brand_name }} cost?

It depends on how many employees you pay and whether you want the web workspace, the desktop app or both. Send a request with your team size and we'll reply with a quote.

How do we get started?

Fill in the request form below. We'll get in touch, set up your company, and email your administrator a link to choose a password. From there you can add employees or upload a workbook.

Do we have to change how we keep our payroll records?

No. You can upload the Excel workbook you use now, or start from our employee roster template.

What happens when GRA changes the PAYE bands?

We add the new rates as a dated version. Runs for months after the change use the new rates. Earlier months keep the rates they were calculated with.

Does approving a run pay my staff?

No. Approval records that the run was checked and agreed. You pay staff through your bank as usual, using the bank listing {{ app_brand_name }} produces.

How do employees receive their payslips?

By email, WhatsApp or SMS, depending on the contact details you hold for them. Every payslip's delivery status shows on the run, and failed ones can be resent.

What's the difference between the web workspace and the desktop app?

The web workspace runs in your browser, and your data is held on our servers. The desktop app runs on one Windows computer, works offline, and keeps the data on that computer.
