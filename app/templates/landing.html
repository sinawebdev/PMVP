Common questions

How much does {{ app_brand_name }} cost?

It depends on how many employees you pay and whether you want the web workspace, the desktop app or both. Send a request with your team size and we'll reply with a quote.

How do we get started?

Fill in the request form below. We'll get in touch, set up your company, and email your administrator a link to choose a password. From there you can add employees or upload a workbook.

Do we have to change how we keep our payroll records?

No. You can upload the Excel workbook you use now, or start from our employee roster template.

What happens when GRA changes the PAYE bands?

We add the new rates as a dated version. Runs for months after the change use the new rates. Earlier months keep the rates they were calculated with.

Does approving a run pay my staff?

No. Approval records that the run was checked and agreed. You pay staff through your bank as usual, using the bank listing {{ app_brand_name }} produces.

How do employees receive their payslips?

By email, WhatsApp or SMS, depending on the contact details you hold for them. Every payslip's delivery status shows on the run, and failed ones can be resent.

What's the difference between the web workspace and the desktop app?

The web workspace runs in your browser, and your data is held on our servers. The desktop app runs on one Windows computer, works offline, and keeps the data on that computer.
