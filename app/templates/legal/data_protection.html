In short: we process employee payroll data only on our clients' instructions, keep it only while the client uses {{ app_brand_name }}, and delete it within 90 days after the contract ends. Employers must keep their own payroll records for six years, so we give each client an export of its data before deleting it.

1. Scope

This policy covers the personal data {{ company_name }} processes in the {{ app_brand_name }} web workspace. It sits alongside our privacy policy, which explains what we collect and why, and our terms of service. Data entered into the {{ app_brand_name }} Desktop app stays on the client's own computer and is not covered here: the client keeps it, backs it up and deletes it.

2. The principles we follow

We handle personal data in line with the data protection principles in Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843):

  • Accountability: we take responsibility for how {{ app_brand_name }} handles personal data, and keep records that show it.
  • Lawful processing: we process data only where the law allows it, and no more than the purpose needs.
  • Specific purpose: payroll data is used for payroll, payslips and the records that go with them, not for anything else.
  • Compatible further use: we don't reuse data for a purpose that doesn't fit why it was collected.
  • Quality: clients can correct employee records at any time, and every change is logged.
  • Openness: we publish what we collect, who we share it with and how long we keep it.
  • Security safeguards: see section 6.
  • Data subject participation: people can see and correct their data, as described in section 7.

3. Our commitments when we process data for a client

For employee payroll data, the client company is the data controller and we are its data processor. As processor, we:

  • process the data only to provide {{ app_brand_name }}, and only on the client's documented instructions
  • make sure everyone at {{ company_name }} with access to it is bound to keep it confidential
  • keep it secure, as described in section 6
  • use only the service providers listed in section 4, and tell clients before we add or replace one
  • help the client answer requests from its employees, and pass on any request we receive directly
  • tell the client without undue delay, and within 72 hours where we can, if we become aware of a breach affecting its data
  • give the client an export of its data on request, and delete it within 90 days after the contract ends

Six-year rule for employers: the Revenue Administration Act, 2016 (Act 915) requires taxpayers, including employers, to keep their records for at least six years. That duty rests with the client, so we provide an export before deleting a client's data. A client can download each payroll run's reports and exports at any time while the contract lasts.

We keep data longer than this schedule only where the law requires it, or where it is needed for an open legal claim or investigation. We delete it once that need ends.

6. Security measures

  • Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS), and data is stored with a database provider that encrypts it at rest.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Repeated failed sign-ins are slowed down and then blocked.
  • Each client sees only its own data, and each user only what their role allows.
  • Every upload, change, approval and send is recorded in an audit trail.
  • Payslip links are signed with their own key, expire, and can be revoked.
  • Only the people who run {{ app_brand_name }} can access production systems, and only when their work requires it.

7. Requests from the people whose data we hold

An employee who wants to see, correct or delete their payroll data should ask their employer, which controls it. If the request comes to us, we pass it to the employer within 5 working days and help them answer it. For data we hold as controller, such as access requests and user accounts, email {{ support_email }}. We check who you are first and reply within 30 days.

8. Breaches

If we find that personal data in {{ app_brand_name }} may have been accessed by someone without permission, we act straight away to contain it. We then tell the affected clients without undue delay. As Act 843 requires, the Data Protection Commission and the people affected are told as soon as reasonably practicable. We record every breach, what was affected, and what we changed as a result.

9. Review

We review this policy at least once a year, and whenever we change how {{ app_brand_name }} stores or shares data. The date at the top shows the last change. Questions: {{ support_email }}.
