1. Scope

This policy covers the personal data {{ company_name }} processes in the {{ app_brand_name }} web workspace. It sits alongside our privacy policy, which explains what we collect and why, and our terms of service, which it forms part of. Data entered into the {{ app_brand_name }} Desktop app stays on the client's own computer and is not covered here: the client keeps it, backs it up and deletes it.

2. The principles we work from

Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) sets out eight data protection principles. This is how we try to apply them:

  • Accountability: we take responsibility for how {{ app_brand_name }} handles personal data, and keep records of key actions.
  • Lawful processing: we aim to process data only where the law allows it, and no more than the purpose needs.
  • Specific purpose: payroll data is used for payroll, payslips and the records that go with them.
  • Compatible further use: we don't reuse data for a purpose that doesn't fit why it was collected.
  • Quality: clients can correct employee records at any time.
  • Openness: we publish what we collect, who we share it with and how long we keep it.
  • Security safeguards: see section 6.
  • Data subject participation: people can ask to see and correct their data, as described in section 7.

3. Our commitments when we process data for a client

For employee payroll data, the client company is the data controller and we are its data processor. As processor, we:

  • process the data to provide {{ app_brand_name }}, following the client's instructions
  • treat it as confidential, and disclose it only where the law requires or our work on the service needs it
  • take the security steps described in section 6
  • use the service providers listed in section 4, and keep that list up to date
  • help the client answer requests from its employees, and pass on any request we receive directly
  • tell the client as soon as reasonably practicable if we have reasonable grounds to believe its data has been accessed without permission
  • give the client an export of its data on request, and normally delete it within 90 days after the contract ends

Six-year rule for employers: the Revenue Administration Act, 2016 (Act 915) requires taxpayers, including employers, to keep their records for at least six years. That duty rests with the client, so a client should keep its own copies and ask us for an export before its contract ends. A client can download each payroll run's reports and exports at any time while the contract lasts.

We may keep data longer than this schedule where the law requires it, or where it is needed for an open legal claim or investigation.

6. Security measures

We take steps we consider reasonable for a service of this kind, including these:

  • Connections to {{ app_brand_name }} use HTTPS. The database is hosted by Supabase, which says it encrypts stored data.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Repeated failed sign-ins can be temporarily blocked.
  • {{ app_brand_name }} is built so that each client sees only its own data, and each user only what their role allows.
  • An audit trail records key actions, such as uploads, approvals and sends.
  • Payslip links are signed with their own key, expire, and can be revoked.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the people who run {{ app_brand_name }}.

No system is perfectly secure, and these steps may change as the service changes.

7. Requests from the people whose data we hold

An employee who wants to see, correct or delete their payroll data should ask their employer, which controls it. If the request comes to us, we pass it to the employer promptly and help them answer it. For data we hold as controller, such as access requests and user accounts, email {{ support_email }}. We check who you are first and reply promptly, within the 40 days Act 843 allows.

8. Breaches

If we have reasonable grounds to believe that personal data in {{ app_brand_name }} has been accessed by someone without permission, we work to contain it and tell the affected clients. As Act 843 requires, the Data Protection Commission and the people affected are told as soon as reasonably practicable.

9. Review

We review this policy from time to time, and when we change how {{ app_brand_name }} stores or shares data. The date at the top shows the last change. Questions: {{ support_email }}.
