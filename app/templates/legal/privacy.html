In short: {{ app_brand_name }} is run by {{ company_name }} in Ghana. When a company uses {{ app_brand_name }} for its payroll, that company decides what happens to its employees' data, and we handle it on the company's behalf. We don't sell personal data, show adverts or use tracking cookies.

1. Who we are

{{ app_brand_name }} ("we", "us") is a payroll service provided by {{ company_name }}, a business based in Ghana. This policy explains what personal data we handle through the {{ app_brand_name }} website, the {{ app_brand_name }} web workspace and the payslip links we send, and what rights you have under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

For questions or requests about your data, email {{ support_email }}.

2. Our two roles

  • On behalf of our clients. A client company (an employer, or a payroll bureau acting for one) adds its employees' details and runs its payroll in {{ app_brand_name }}. The client is the data controller for that data. We process it only to provide {{ app_brand_name }} to that client, and only on its instructions. If you work for one of our clients, your employer is your first point of contact; see section 9.
  • For our own purposes. We are the data controller for the details you send us when you request access, for the user accounts we create, and for the records we keep to secure the service.

3. What we collect

When you request access

Your company name, your name and your work email. If you choose to give them, also your phone number, town or city, number of employees and a message.

When you have a {{ app_brand_name }} account

Your name, email address, role and company. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash, so nobody at {{ app_brand_name }} can read it. We also record when you were invited and when you activated your account.

Security and activity records

Each sign-in and sign-out is recorded with your IP address and browser type. The audit trail records who uploaded, changed, approved or sent what, and when.

Payroll data we process for clients

Depending on what the client provides, this can include:

  • names and contact details: full name, phone number and email address
  • government identifiers: Ghana Card number, SSNIT number and TIN
  • payment details: bank name, branch and account number, or mobile money number
  • pay information: salary, allowances, overtime, deductions, PAYE, SSNIT contributions, net pay and payslips
  • the payroll workbooks the client uploads, kept as they were uploaded
  • expense records and receipts
  • payslip delivery records: the email address or phone number used, the channel, and whether delivery succeeded

Technical data

Our hosting and network providers keep server logs, which include IP addresses and details of each request, for security and troubleshooting.

4. How we use it

  • To reply to access requests and set up client companies, which are steps you have asked us to take before a contract.
  • To provide {{ app_brand_name }} under our contract with the client: calculating payroll, producing reports, and delivering payslips by email, WhatsApp or SMS.
  • To keep the service secure: limiting repeated sign-in attempts, investigating misuse and keeping an audit trail.
  • To send service messages, such as account invitations and notices about changes to {{ app_brand_name }}.
  • To meet our legal obligations, for example answering a lawful request from a court or regulator.

We don't sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to make automated decisions about people. Payroll is calculated from the client's figures and the statutory rates, and the client reviews and approves every run.

6. Data stored outside Ghana

The {{ app_brand_name }} database is hosted in Ireland and the application runs in the United States, so data you or your employer give us leaves Ghana. We use established providers that commit by contract to keep the data secure and to use it only for the service they provide to us, and we transfer only what {{ app_brand_name }} needs to work.

7. How long we keep it

A client's payroll data is kept while the client uses {{ app_brand_name }}. When the contract ends, we give the client an export of it on request, and we delete it within 90 days. Access requests that don't lead to a contract are deleted within 12 months. The full schedule is in our data protection and retention policy.

8. How we protect it

  • Every connection to {{ app_brand_name }} is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and repeated failed sign-ins are slowed down and then blocked.
  • Each client company sees only its own data, and each user sees only what their role allows.
  • An audit trail records every upload, change, approval and send.
  • Payslip links are signed and can be revoked. They expire after 5 days, or 30 days for links sent by SMS.
  • Access to the production systems is limited to the people who need it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts personal data at risk, we will tell the affected clients without delay and, as Act 843 requires, notify the Data Protection Commission and the people affected.

9. Your rights

Under Act 843 you can ask us:

  • whether we hold personal data about you, and for a copy of it
  • to correct data that is wrong or incomplete
  • to delete data, or to stop or limit how we use it, where the law allows

If you work for one of our clients, your employer controls your payroll data, so please ask them first. If you contact us instead, we will pass your request to your employer and help them answer it.

Anyone else can email {{ support_email }}. We will check who you are before we release any data, and reply within 30 days.

You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana at dataprotection.org.gh.

10. Cookies

We use one essential cookie. It keeps you signed in and protects our forms against forged requests. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies. Your browser also notes, for the current visit only, that the {{ app_brand_name }} logo animation has already played, so it doesn't replay on every page. That note stays on your device.

11. The Windows app

{{ app_brand_name }} Desktop keeps payroll data on your own computer, not on our servers, and checks its licence without going online. We don't receive the payroll data you enter in it. The installer is downloaded from GitHub, which handles that download under its own privacy statement.

12. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If a change matters, we tell client administrators by email or in {{ app_brand_name }} before it takes effect.
