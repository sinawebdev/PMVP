In short: {{ app_brand_name }} is run by {{ company_name }} in Ghana. When a company uses {{ app_brand_name }} for its payroll, that company decides what happens to its employees' data, and we handle it on the company's behalf. We don't sell personal data, show adverts or use tracking cookies.

1. Who we are

{{ app_brand_name }} ("we", "us") is a payroll service provided by {{ company_name }}, a business based in Ghana{% if company_address %}, at {{ company_address }}{% endif %}. This policy explains what personal data we handle through the {{ app_brand_name }} website, the {{ app_brand_name }} web workspace and the payslip links we send, and what rights you have under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

For questions or requests about your data, email {{ support_email }}.

2. Our two roles

  • On behalf of our clients. A client company (an employer, or a payroll bureau acting for one) adds its employees' details and runs its payroll in {{ app_brand_name }}. The client is the data controller for that data. We process it to provide {{ app_brand_name }} to that client, following its instructions. If you work for one of our clients, your employer is your first point of contact; see section 9.
  • For our own purposes. We are the data controller for the details you send us when you request access, for the user accounts we create, and for the records we keep to secure the service.

3. What we collect

When you request access

Your company name, your name, your work email and what you are interested in. We need these to reply, and we can't deal with your request without them. You can also choose to give your phone number, town or city, number of employees and a message. If we issue a trial licence for the Windows app, we keep a record of it.

When you have a {{ app_brand_name }} account

Your name, email address, role and company. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash, so it can't be read back. We also record when you were invited and when you activated your account.

Security and activity records

Each sign-in and sign-out is recorded with your IP address and browser type. The audit trail records key actions, such as uploads, approvals and sends, with who did them and when.

Payroll data we process for clients

Depending on what the client provides, this can include:

  • names and contact details: full name, phone number and email address
  • government identifiers: Ghana Card number, SSNIT number and TIN
  • payment details: bank name, branch and account number, or mobile money number
  • pay information: salary, allowances, overtime, deductions, PAYE, SSNIT contributions, net pay and payslips
  • the payroll workbooks the client uploads
  • expense records and receipts
  • payslip delivery records: the email address or phone number used, the channel, and the delivery status reported to us

Technical data

Our hosting and network providers keep server logs, which include IP addresses and details of each request, for security and troubleshooting.

4. How we use it

  • To reply to access requests and set up client companies, which are steps you have asked us to take before a contract.
  • To provide {{ app_brand_name }} to the client: calculating payroll, producing reports, and delivering payslips.
  • To keep the service secure: limiting repeated sign-in attempts, investigating misuse and keeping an audit trail.
  • To send service messages, such as account invitations, licences and notices about changes to {{ app_brand_name }}.
  • To meet our legal obligations, for example answering a lawful request from a court or regulator.

We don't sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to make automated decisions about people. Payroll is calculated from the client's figures and the statutory rates we hold, and the client reviews and approves each run.

6. Data stored outside Ghana

The {{ app_brand_name }} database is hosted in Ireland and the application runs in the United States, and some of our other providers work on global networks. So data you or your employer give us leaves Ghana. We choose established providers, and we try to send them only what {{ app_brand_name }} needs to work.

7. How long we keep it

A client's payroll data is kept while the client uses {{ app_brand_name }}. When the contract ends, we give the client an export of it on request, and we normally delete it within 90 days. Access requests that don't lead to a contract are normally deleted within 12 months. The full schedule is in our data protection and retention policy.

8. How we protect it

We take steps we consider reasonable for a service of this kind, including these:

  • Connections to {{ app_brand_name }} use HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and repeated failed sign-ins can be temporarily blocked.
  • {{ app_brand_name }} is built so that each client company sees only its own data, and each user only what their role allows. The people who run {{ app_brand_name }} can see client data when their work on the service requires it.
  • An audit trail records key actions, such as uploads, approvals and sends.
  • Payslip links are signed and can be revoked. They expire after 5 days, or 30 days for links sent by SMS. Until then, anyone who has a link can open that payslip, so please treat it like the payslip itself.
  • Access to the production systems is limited.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't promise that data will never be lost or accessed without permission. If we have reasonable grounds to believe that personal data has been accessed by someone without permission, we will tell the affected clients and, as Act 843 requires, notify the Data Protection Commission and the people affected as soon as reasonably practicable.

9. Your rights

Under Act 843 you can ask us:

  • whether we hold personal data about you, and for a copy of it
  • to correct data that is wrong or incomplete
  • to delete data, or to stop or limit how we use it, where the law allows

If you work for one of our clients, your employer controls your payroll data, so please ask them first. If you contact us instead, we will pass your request to your employer and help them answer it.

Anyone else can email {{ support_email }}. We will check who you are before we release any data, and reply promptly, within the 40 days Act 843 allows.

You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana at dpc.gov.gh.

10. Cookies

We use one essential cookie. It keeps you signed in and protects our forms against forged requests. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies. Your browser also notes, for the current visit only, that the {{ app_brand_name }} logo animation has already played, so it doesn't replay on every page. That note stays on your device.

11. The Windows app

{{ app_brand_name }} Desktop keeps payroll data on your own computer, not on our servers, and checks its licence without going online. We don't receive the payroll data you enter in it. The installer is downloaded from GitHub, which handles that download under its own privacy statement.

12. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If a change matters, we aim to tell client administrators by email or in {{ app_brand_name }}.
